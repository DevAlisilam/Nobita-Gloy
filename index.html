About

One provider for every site requirement.

Operating Principle — The Three Ps
Patience · Persistence · Perspiration
Client relationships are treated as long-term, and site issues get handled directly rather than passed along a chain.

Nobility Glory Company Ltd. is an industrial support and services provider based in Umluj, Tabuk Region, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Sumon Rayhan founded the company in 2016 and built it from a single service offering into the five-line operation it runs today. The client list has grown alongside it — now spanning 17 contractor relationships that include Petrofac, Samsung C&T, Sinohydro, and Larsen & Toubro, on projects tied to NEOM, the Red Sea Project, Saudi Aramco contractor networks, and Royal Commission developments.

The five core service lines — manpower supply, heavy equipment rental, fuel supply, scrap collection and recycling, and catering — run through a single point of contact for each client. A project manager coordinating five vendors separately loses time at every handoff; a project manager working with Nobility Glory does not carry that overhead.

Site issues get handled directly rather than routed through account layers, and client relationships stay active well past the closing of any single contract — the reason repeat business makes up a meaningful share of the company's project load.

FAQ

Common questions

What services does Nobility Glory Company Ltd. provide? +
We provide five core services: manpower supply, heavy equipment rental, fuel supply, scrap collection and recycling, and catering services — all coordinated through a single point of contact.
Which areas of Saudi Arabia does Nobility Glory serve? +
Our primary coverage spans Tabuk Region, NEOM project area, Duba, Amaala, Al Wajh, the Red Sea Project, and Umluj, with extended support across Jubail, Rabigh, and Al Qassim.
How fast can Nobility Glory deliver fuel to a site? +
Fuel delivery timelines depend on site location and volume requirements. We typically coordinate delivery within 24-48 hours for established project sites within our primary coverage area.
What kind of workforce does Nobility Glory supply? +
We supply skilled and semi-skilled labor for construction and industrial projects, including equipment operators, general laborers, technicians, and support staff — all vetted and compliant with Saudi labor regulations.
Does Nobility Glory work with international contractors? +
Yes. Our client base includes international EPC contractors such as Petrofac, Samsung C&T, Sinohydro, and Larsen & Toubro, among others working on major Saudi developments.
What happens if a piece of rented equipment breaks down on site? +
We maintain a contingency plan for equipment failures. Replacement equipment is dispatched promptly to minimize downtime, and our project manager coordinates directly with your site team.
How is Nobility Glory different from working with five separate vendors? +
A single point of contact eliminates handoff delays between vendors. One project manager coordinates all five service lines, reducing administrative overhead and ensuring faster response times on site.
Is Nobility Glory a licensed company in Saudi Arabia? +
Yes. Nobility Glory Company Ltd. holds Commercial Registration No. 7054099101, registered in Umluj, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and our workforce is registered under Qiwa in compliance with Saudi labor law.
