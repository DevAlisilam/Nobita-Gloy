FAQ

Common questions

What major projects has Nobility Glory worked on? +
Nobility Glory has provided manpower and site support to NEOM, the Red Sea Project, Saudi Aramco contractor projects, Royal Commission developments, and several water and power infrastructure projects including Yanbu Phase 3, Jubail 3(B), Rabigh 3, and SEPCO3.
Does Nobility Glory work as a main contractor or a support provider? +
Nobility Glory works as a support provider, supplying manpower, equipment, fuel, and site services to the contractor teams running these projects, rather than acting as the primary contractor.
What sectors does Nobility Glory have project experience in? +
Project experience spans giga-project development, oil and gas, water infrastructure and desalination, power generation, and regional infrastructure development.
Can Nobility Glory support a project similar in scale to NEOM or the Red Sea Project? +
Yes. Nobility Glory has already supported contractor teams on giga-project-scale developments and can share specific project experience relevant to a new site on request.
