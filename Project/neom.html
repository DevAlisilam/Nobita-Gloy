Project Overview

NEOM is one of the Kingdom's largest giga-project developments, spanning multiple zones across Tabuk Province. Nobility Glory has supported contractor teams operating within the development with manpower deployment and site services, working under the contractor teams executing the build rather than as NEOM's direct contractor.

Project Description

As a support provider, Nobility Glory has mobilized workforce and site services to contractor teams operating within the NEOM development. Support includes manpower deployment, equipment access coordination, and site logistics, delivered in coordination with the contractor teams running the work.

NEOM's scale requires support providers who understand giga-project logistics: remote site access, large-scale workforce accommodation, and mobilization on short notice. Nobility Glory's experience across comparable regional developments has built operational familiarity with the coordination and safety standards a site of this size requires.
