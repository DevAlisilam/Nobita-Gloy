The Service

Fuel that reaches the site, not just the nearest station.

A generator or a fleet of site equipment running low on diesel doesn't wait for a convenient delivery window. Nobility Glory delivers diesel and gasoline directly to project sites, camps, farms, and factories, with confirmed orders moving within 24 hours. The tanker fleet ranges from 6,000 to 32,000 liters, so a small camp top-up and a large project order both get a tanker sized to the job rather than an oversized or undersized delivery.

Core coverage runs across Tabuk, the NEOM project area, Duba, Amaala, Al Wajh, the Red Sea Project, and Umluj, including remote and desert locations that sit well outside a normal delivery route. Fuel delivery extends beyond these areas to sites across Saudi Arabia on request.
