Company Profile

One partner, five services, built for the Kingdom's giga-projects.

Nobility Glory Company Ltd. is a Saudi-registered enterprise operating five integrated service lines across the Kingdom's largest industrial developments: manpower supply, heavy equipment rental, fuel supply, scrap collection and recycling, and camp catering.

Founded in 2016 by Sumon Rayhan, the company was built on a straightforward premise: EPC contractors shouldn't have to stitch together five separate vendors to cover what a single site actually needs to run. Every service line reports through one accountable relationship, not five separate contracts, wherever in the Kingdom that project sits.

Message from the CEO
"Five services, one accountable partner."

"I started Nobility Glory in 2016 because every EPC contractor I spoke with in Tabuk Region managed the same headache: five different vendors for five different needs, none of them talking to each other. We built one company to close that gap."

Since then, that founding idea has grown into a workforce of skilled personnel, an owned equipment fleet, and active relationships across seventeen client organizations working the Kingdom's largest developments.

Every project we take on — whether it's manpower for a desalination plant or fuel delivery to a remote site — runs on the same standard: certified people, documented processes, and a single point of accountability.

Our Mission

Operational reliability, one contract at a time.

To give EPC contractors on the Kingdom's largest developments a single, accountable source for the labor, equipment, fuel, waste recovery and catering their sites depend on — without the coordination cost of managing five separate vendors.

  • Five services, one point of contact
  • Positioned close to NEOM and the Red Sea Project
  • Documented process across every service line
Our Vision

To be the Kingdom's most complete operational partner for EPC contractors, wherever the work is.

We aim to be the vendor a contractor calls first — not because we're the largest name in the Kingdom, but because we're the most complete partner for what a site actually needs to run, on any project we take on.

  • Depth across five services, not scale in just one
  • Growth aligned with Vision 2030's industrial goals
  • Built for giga-projects, not positioned as a regional alternative to them
